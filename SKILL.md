# how i write emails

- keep it short, like 3-4 lines max
- start with "hi <their first name>," never "dear"
- no "hope this email finds you well" or any of that fluff
- if im saying no, be nice about it but clear
- if i need time just say ill get back to them by a specific day
- end with just "thanks" on its own line, nothing fancy
- never promise money, dates or meetings i havent confirmed, leave a [check] note for me instead
